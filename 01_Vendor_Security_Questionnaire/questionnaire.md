# Vendor Security Assessment Questionnaire

## Overview
This questionnaire is designed to evaluate the security posture, data protection practices, and compliance controls of third-party vendors seeking to provide services or access internal systems.

---

## 1. Access Control & Authentication
* **1.1** Do you enforce Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for all administrative and privileged access to systems containing company data? *(Yes / No / Comments)*
* **1.2** Is access provisioned based on the Principle of Least Privilege and reviewed at least quarterly? *(Yes / No / Comments)*

---

## 2. Data Protection & Encryption
* **2.1** Is all company data encrypted at rest using industry-standard algorithms (e.g., AES-256)? *(Yes / No / Comments)*
* **2.2** Is all data encrypted in transit across public networks using modern protocols (e.g., TLS 1.3)? *(Yes / No / Comments)*

---

## 3. Compliance & Audit
* **3.1** Do you undergo an annual independent third-party audit resulting in a SOC 2 Type II report or ISO/IEC 27001 certification? *(Yes / No / Comments)*
* **3.2** Can you provide a summary of your most recent security assessment or penetration test upon request? *(Yes / No / Comments)*

---

## 4. Incident Response & Business Continuity
* **4.1** Do you maintain a documented Incident Response Plan that is tested at least annually? *(Yes / No / Comments)*
* **4.2** What is your strict Service Level Agreement (SLA) or commitment for notifying customers after confirming a security incident or data breach? *(Specify hours)*
